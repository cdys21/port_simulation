What this view teaches well A single crane loss or surprise vessel makes the dots physically spread into quay, yard, gate, and rail zones. The user sees pressure accumulate instead of inferring it.

Best uses

Teaching accumulation Users immediately understand what congestion means because they can see it occupy space.
Explaining yard policy Great fit if stacking logic and retrieval slowdown are central to the product story.
Weaker for fine resource logic More abstract around individual cranes and berth sequencing than Control Tower.

Example interventions

Add vessel More blue dots land in yard, then orange dots rise at the truck side later.
Remove crane Quay-ready export dots stop clearing, and risk dots spread into the side panels.
Open gate overtime Truck queue dots thin first, then yard block density slowly relaxes.
